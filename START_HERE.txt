BREATHE & BLOOM — iPHONE SAFE SETUP

This is a Safari Home Screen version, not an IPA/App Store app. It does not require Developer Mode, profiles, jailbreaks, or connecting to the iPhone.

1) Read README.md for the free GitHub Pages hosting steps.
2) Publish the files over HTTPS.
3) Open the link in Safari on the iPhone and TEST it as a website first.
4) When satisfied: Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen > enable Open as Web App (if shown) > Add.

Important: the published webpage/repository is public. Do not upload private information. The app stores practice progress locally on the device.
